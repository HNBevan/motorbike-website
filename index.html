Our Workshop
"Every bike we build is a conversation between material, motion, and the person who will ride it."

Iron Vale was founded in 2009 in a rented arch under the Hackney overground. What started as a single builder restoring old British bikes has grown into a six-person workshop producing some of the most refined custom motorcycles in the country.

We believe that London, gritty, fast and relentless, is the ideal city to build performance motorcycles. That tension between refinement and rawness is at the heart of everything we make.

Each build takes between four and eight months. Each one is yours from the first sketch to the final test ride. We don't build bikes in batches. We build yours.

James Calloway
Founder & Master Fabricator
